COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about document preparation, legal validity, and data privacy.

Is a document created on LexDraft legally binding?
Documents generated on LexDraft conform to standard statutory requirements. To make your document fully legally binding, you must print the PDF and sign it in the presence of required disinterested witnesses and a Notary Public as mandated by your local state laws.
Can I edit my document after downloading the PDF?
Yes! All wizard progress is auto-saved to your local dashboard session. You can return to your dashboard at any time, update details (such as adding new beneficiaries or changing executors), and re-generate a fresh PDF.
Is my personal legal information kept private?
Your data is saved directly in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). We do not sell, monetize, or transmit your confidential estate details to third parties.
What happens if I leave the wizard mid-way?
Your form progress is automatically debounced and saved. Returning to the site or clicking "Continue Draft" on your Client Dashboard restores your exact step and previously entered input fields.
Do I need an attorney to use this software?
LexDraft is a self-guided legal document compiler. For straightforward estate planning and sworn statements, our guided wizard provides structured templates. For complex trusts or contested estates, we recommend consulting a licensed attorney.
How long does it take to complete a document?
Most users complete a Last Will & Testament, Power of Attorney, or Sworn Affidavit in 10 to 15 minutes.
