PRODUCT #1

Last Will & Testament

Launch Will Wizard →

A Last Will & Testament is an essential legal instrument that dictates how your estate, real property, financial accounts, and personal belongings should be handled after your passing. It enables you to nominate a trusted personal representative (Executor) to oversee probate court procedures.

Key Included Legal Clauses:

  • Testator Identity & Sound Mind Affirmation
  • Explicit Prior Will & Codicil Revocation Clause
  • Executor & Successor Executor Appointments
  • Primary & Contingent Beneficiary Share Allocation
  • Guardianship Nomination for Minor Children
  • Funeral, Cremation & Memorial Wishes
  • Two Disinterested Witnesses Signature Attestation

Who This Is For:

  • ✓ Homeowners & real property owners
  • ✓ Married couples & parents with minor children
  • ✓ Individuals seeking clear executor designation
  • ✓ Anyone wanting to prevent state intestacy asset splits
PRODUCT #2

Durable Power of Attorney

Launch POA Wizard →

A Power of Attorney (POA) grants your chosen Agent or Attorney-in-Fact legal authority to make financial, real estate, and healthcare decisions on your behalf if you become temporarily or permanently incapacitated due to illness or injury.

Key Included Legal Clauses:

  • Financial & Banking Management Authority
  • Real Estate & Business Transaction Powers
  • Durable Status Provision (retains power during incapacity)
  • Healthcare Choices & End-of-Life Medical Directives
  • Successor Agent Appointment Provisions
  • Notary Public Official Acknowledgment Block

Who This Is For:

  • ✓ Adults wanting emergency financial management protection
  • ✓ Individuals establishing healthcare advance directives
  • ✓ Small business owners appointing operational agents
  • ✓ Seniors planning long-term care management
PRODUCT #3

Sworn Affidavit

Launch Affidavit Wizard →

A Sworn Affidavit is a written statement of facts voluntarily made under an oath or affirmation administered by an authorized Notary Public. It carries legal weight under penalties of perjury.

Key Included Legal Clauses:

  • Jurisdiction State & County Header
  • Affiant Identity & Oath Declaration
  • Numbered Paragraph Statement of Facts
  • Penalty of Perjury Confirmation Clause
  • Notary Public Jurat Signature Block

Who This Is For:

  • ✓ Proof of residency or address confirmation
  • ✓ Name change or identity discrepancy statements
  • ✓ Financial disclosures & debt statements
  • ✓ Insurance loss or damage claims under oath
