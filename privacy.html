Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Local Storage & Data Privacy

LexDraft stores user document drafts directly in browser local storage (`localStorage`). We do not collect, monetize, or sell personal legal data to third parties.

2. Non-Lawyer Self-Service Disclaimer

LexDraft provides automated software solutions for legal document preparation. LexDraft is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal representation.

3. Document Storage & Security

All data entered into LexDraft wizards is encrypted in browser storage. Generated PDFs are constructed entirely on your local machine using client-side JavaScript.
