1. Overview & Data Ownership Philosophy

At LexDraft Systems, we operate under a foundational principle: Your legal documents and personal affairs belong exclusively to you. We have architected our software platform from the ground up to operate with zero server-side storage of draft legal documents.

🔒 Zero-Knowledge Guarantee: Unlike traditional web applications that upload your sensitive family, beneficiary, and asset information to cloud servers, LexDraft processes all form data locally within your browser's private memory.

Whether you are drafting a Last Will & Testament, nominating a Financial Power of Attorney, or formalizing a Sworn Affidavit, your input never touches external analytics, ad tracking networks, or remote storage databases.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To maintain standard compliance and maximize client data security, LexDraft strictly enforces a policy of non-collection for sensitive draft records:

  • No Estate Data Aggregation: We do not log or view asset values, real estate properties, bank account details, or beneficiary designations.
  • No Personal ID Tracking: Social Security Numbers, Passport Numbers, and Government Identification details are never harvested.
  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize any user metadata or document activity to credit bureaus, ad providers, or insurance brokers.
  • No Telemetry on Document Content: The text entered into textboxes and wizard steps stays strictly local.

3. Client-Side Browser Storage (`localStorage`)

LexDraft utilizes standard Web Storage APIs (`localStorage` and `sessionStorage`) to provide real-time auto-saving functionality as you navigate document wizards:

How LocalStorage Works: When you type answers into a LexDraft wizard, the data is instantly saved in your web browser's isolated local domain storage. Closing or refreshing your tab will not lose your progress.

Because `localStorage` resides on your device hardware, clearing your browser history or cache will wipe all saved document drafts unless you have exported them as PDF or JSON backups.

4. Cookies & Local Preference Storage

LexDraft uses minimal functional cookies and session tokens strictly required for website operation:

  • Functional Session Tokens: Used to track active account login sessions when accessing the Client Dashboard.
  • RTL & Display Preferences: Stored locally to remember your layout preferences (such as Right-to-Left script direction).
  • Zero 3rd-Party Tracking Cookies: We do not embed cross-site advertising cookies or retargeting scripts (such as Facebook Pixel or Google Ads trackers).

5. Client Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA Alignment)

Under global privacy standards including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain total rights over your digital footprint:

Right to Access & Portability

You can view, copy, or export your complete saved document data at any time via instant client-side PDF download.

Right to Total Erasure

Clearing your browser's site data or clicking "Reset Draft" permanently erases all trace of your document data immediately.

6. Client Security & Data Protection Protocols

While LexDraft implements browser-level encryption standards, users are encouraged to maintain basic endpoint security:

  • Avoid using public or shared computers when inputting confidential asset or beneficiary data.
  • Ensure your operating system and web browser are updated with recent security patches.
  • Always log out of your LexDraft Client Dashboard session when finished.
