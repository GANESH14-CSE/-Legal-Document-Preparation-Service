STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

The Complete Document Creation Journey

Follow our 9-step guided workflow engineered for clarity, accuracy, and ease of use.

Step 01

Choose Document

Select between a Last Will & Testament, Power of Attorney, or Sworn Affidavit.

Step 02

Personal Details

Enter full legal name, permanent residence address, and state jurisdiction.

Step 03

Family & Beneficiaries

List primary and contingent beneficiaries receiving asset shares.

Step 04

Assets & Property

Specify real estate, bank accounts, investments, and personal property.

Step 05

Executor / Attorney Details

Appoint personal executors, guardians for minors, or agents-in-fact.

Step 06

Special Instructions

Add specific bequest instructions, debt provisions, or funeral requests.

Step 07

Review Information

Verify all entered details on a comprehensive summary checklist before compiling.

Step 08

Finalize Draft

Lock in your entries and auto-save the document state to your client dashboard.

Step 09

PDF Generation

Download a clean, printable PDF ready for signature, witnessing, and notarization.
