OUR MISSION

The Problem & Our Solution

Traditional legal document preparation has often felt intimidating, expensive, and confusing. Blank forms leave people guessing what information is required.

LexDraft breaks complex statutory documents down into intuitive step-by-step questions. Users can draft, review, and auto-save progress directly in browser storage before generating printable PDFs.
