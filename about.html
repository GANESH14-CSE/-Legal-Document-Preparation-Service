THE MOTIVATION

Why Did We Start LexDraft?

We founded LexDraft after witnessing millions of families delay vital estate planning simply because traditional legal services were prohibitively expensive and intimidating.

Overcoming Cost Barriers

Traditional law firm rates made basic estate planning inaccessible to the average household. We set out to make self-guided legal tools 100% accessible.

Demystifying Legalese

Raw PDF forms left people guessing what details were required. We built plain-language guided prompts to provide total clarity at every step.

Restoring Data Privacy

Personal asset details belong to you. We designed a privacy-first local browser storage architecture that keeps data safely on your device.

OUR JOURNEY
